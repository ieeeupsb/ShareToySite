How it works
  1. A QR code holds about 2.9 KB, so the image is shrunk to fit.
  2. The QR holds a link to this page plus the image data.
  3. Any phone camera opens the link and shows the image.

Runs entirely in your browser. The image lives inside the QR code, not on a server.
